How Diamond Rating Works

Every player starts with a 1000 Diamond Rating. Your rating represents your overall performance in Daily Diamond.

Your rating changes after every game based on your result, number of guesses, hints used, completion time, and current rating.


Rating Formula

Your rating change is calculated using:

Rating Change = (Base Score × Guess Modifier × Hint Modifier × Completion Modifier) × Rating Multiplier


Base Score

The result of your game determines the starting rating value.

💎 Win: +100 Base Score
❌ Give Up: -50 Base Score
⏳ Unfinished Game: -25 Rating Penalty


Guess Modifier

Fewer guesses are rewarded with a larger rating increase. The effect decreases gradually as guesses increase.

Guess Modifier = 1.50 - (log10(Guesses ÷ 5) × 0.50)

The minimum Guess Modifier is 0.70x.

Examples:
5 guesses: 1.50x
20 guesses: ~1.20x
50 guesses: ~1.00x
100 guesses: ~0.85x
200+ guesses: 0.70x


Hint Modifier

Using fewer hints gives a larger rating reward. Hints have diminishing impact, meaning early hints matter more than additional hints later.

Hint Modifier = 1.25 - (log10(Hints + 1) × 0.50)

The minimum Hint Modifier is 0.50x.

Examples:
0 hints: 1.25x
1 hint: ~1.10x
5 hints: ~0.86x
10 hints: ~0.73x
20+ hints: 0.50x


Completion Modifier

Completing a game on the same day gives a small bonus.

Same-day completion: 1.00x
Completed later: 0.95x


Rating Multiplier

Your current rating affects how quickly your rating changes.

Higher-rated players gain rating more slowly, while lower-rated players can climb faster.

This keeps the leaderboard competitive and prevents ratings from increasing too quickly.


Rating Progression

Your Diamond Rating is updated after every completed game by adding or subtracting your rating change.

Example:

Starting Rating: 1000
Game Rating Change: +65
New Rating: 1065

Playing more games does not automatically increase your rating. Every game is another opportunity to gain or lose rating.


Ranking

Players are ranked by their current Diamond Rating.

The highest rating appears at the top of the leaderboard.

A player with a 1500 rating ranks above a player with a 1200 rating.


The goal is to build the highest Diamond Rating by consistently winning games, solving puzzles efficiently, and minimizing hints.
