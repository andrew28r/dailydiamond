How Diamond Rating Works

Every player starts with a 1000 Diamond Rating. Your rating shows your overall performance in Daily Diamond.


How Your Rating Changes

After each game, your Diamond Rating will change based on your result.

💎 Winning increases your rating.
❌ Losing or giving up decreases your rating.
⏳ Unfinished games receive a small rating penalty.


Rating Formula

Your rating change is calculated using:

Rating Change = Base Score × Guess Modifier × Hint Modifier


Base Score
Determines the starting amount of rating gained or lost based on the result of the game.

A win gives a positive rating change.
A loss or give up gives a negative rating change.
An unfinished game receives a smaller penalty.


Guess Modifier
Rewards players who solve games using fewer guesses.

Fewer guesses = bigger rating increase.
More guesses = smaller rating increase.


Hint Modifier
Adjusts your rating based on how many hints you use.

Using fewer hints increases your reward.
Using more hints reduces your rating gain.


Ranking

Players are ranked by their current Diamond Rating.

A player with a higher rating ranks above players with a lower rating.

Example:

#1 Player - 1500 Rating
#2 Player - 1250 Rating
#3 Player - 1100 Rating


Playing More Games

Playing more games does not automatically increase your Diamond Rating.

Every completed game affects your rating, so consistent performance over time is what matters.

A player who wins many games efficiently will continue to increase their rating, while a player who loses or uses many hints may see their rating decrease.


Rating Progression

As your rating increases, climbing higher becomes more difficult because you must continue performing well to maintain your rank.

Your Diamond Rating represents your long-term performance, not just your best single game.


The goal is to build the highest Diamond Rating by consistently winning games and solving them efficiently.
