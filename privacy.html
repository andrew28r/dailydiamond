Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Daily Diamond collects information needed to provide game features and maintain player progress. This may include:

  • Player ID or username
  • Game progress and completed games
  • Wins, streaks, ratings, and leaderboard information
  • Game statistics used to improve the experience

How We Use Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Save player progress
  • Display leaderboards and ratings
  • Track game statistics
  • Improve and maintain Daily Diamond

Data Storage

Daily Diamond uses Supabase to store player information and game data. Supabase provides the database services used to maintain player accounts, scores, and game history.


Advertising

Daily Diamond uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide personalized advertisements, measure ad performance, and improve advertising relevance.

Users may manage or disable personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings.


Cookies

Cookies may be used by advertising partners and analytics services to provide and improve services. Daily Diamond does not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information.


Third-Party Services

Daily Diamond uses third-party services including Google AdSense and Supabase. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.
