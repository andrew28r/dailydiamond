Privacy Policy

I created Daily Diamond as a free game for baseball fans. This page explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information Collected

When you use Daily Diamond, the site may store information such as your player ID, game progress, guesses, wins, streaks, ratings, and leaderboard information.

How Information Is Used

This information is used only to provide game features such as saving progress, tracking statistics, and displaying leaderboards.

Data Storage

Daily Diamond uses Supabase to store player data and game information.

Advertising

Daily Diamond uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to provide personalized ads and measure advertising performance.

You can manage your advertising preferences through Google's advertising settings.
